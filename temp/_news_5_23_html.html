A Panel Bender is a modern sheet metal fabrication system, used for high-precision bending of pieces. "Panel benders differ from standard press brakes in that they use synchronized upper and lower bending blades, coupled with advanced technology, to accommodate high-volume productivity of challenging, multi-angle workpieces.
