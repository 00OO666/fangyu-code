Who We Are

FANGYU is a premier international trading company specializing in industrial machine tools, sheet metal processing equipment, precision accessories, and tooling solutions for manufacturing enterprises worldwide.

Strategically positioned at the heart of China's advanced manufacturing ecosystem, we connect global clients with cutting-edge machinery through our network of 50+ leading CNC machine tool manufacturers.

From initial consultation to equipment delivery and beyond, we ensure seamless service with our signature 48-hour global on-site support guarantee — keeping your production lines running at peak performance.
